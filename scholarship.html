Financial Assistance

We provide practical support to help families meet essential educational and healthcare needs with dignity and care.

Scholarship Assistance

Support for education and academic growth.

This assistance helps students and learners cover tuition, school fees, books, and other educational costs so they can continue learning with confidence.

  • Tuition and school fees support
  • Study materials and exam expenses
  • Academic mentoring and school follow-up
💊
Medical Assistance

Support for urgent healthcare needs.

We help individuals and families access healthcare support during times of medical need, with emphasis on emergency care and treatment access.

  • Medical treatment support
  • Emergency healthcare referrals
  • Hospital and medication assistance
